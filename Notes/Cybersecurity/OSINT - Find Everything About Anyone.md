## Summary
Comprehensive OSINT (Open Source Intelligence) guide covering tools and techniques for finding information about anyone online. Tools mentioned: IntelTechniques, Epieos (email lookup), Sherlock (username hunting across platforms), Holehe (email verification), Hunchly (web investigation), Overpass Turbo (OpenStreetMap queries), OpenStreetMap (geolocation), SpiderFoot (automated OSINT). Topics: digital identity connection, username hunting, email intelligence, geolocation, evidence collection.

## Key Takeaways
- OSINT leverages publicly available data to build comprehensive profiles — starting with just a username or email can lead to uncovering a person's full digital footprint
- Specialized tools like Sherlock, Holehe, Epieos, and SpiderFoot automate the tedious work of cross-referencing platforms and data sources
- Geolocation (OpenStreetMap, Overpass Turbo) and metadata from images/photos can reveal physical locations and movement patterns
- IntelTechniques provides a unified interface for many OSINT operations, making it a good starting point
- Evidence collection and proper documentation are critical for OSINT findings to be useful in investigations
