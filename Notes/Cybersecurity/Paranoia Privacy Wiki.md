## Summary
Free comprehensive guide to digital privacy and anonymity. Covers secure browsing (Tor, VPNs, browser hardening), encrypted messaging (Signal, Session), phone hardening (de-Googling, app permissions), metadata removal from photos/documents, private email services, privacy-friendly app alternatives, and anonymous payment methods. Essential reference for anyone concerned about digital privacy.

## Key Takeaways
- Privacy is a spectrum — the wiki helps you choose your threat model and pick the right tools for your specific needs
- Secure browsing goes beyond just using a VPN — Tor, browser hardening, and de-Googling your phone are all part of a comprehensive approach
- Metadata removal from photos and documents is often overlooked but critical for preventing information leakage
- Encrypted messaging options like Signal and Session each have different trade-offs in terms of convenience vs. anonymity
- The guide covers anonymous payments, which is the often-forgotten piece of the privacy puzzle
