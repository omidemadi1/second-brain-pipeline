## Summary

Tom's Hardware article explaining why you need a macro pad even if you have a mouse with 19 buttons. Covers options from $10 mechanical numpads to multi-screen window management setups. The article argues that dedicated macro pads add more utility than button-heavy mice because they offer labeled, customizable keys for repeated workflows (window snapping, app launches, text snippets).

## Key Takeaways

- Macro pads range from ~$10 mechanical numpads to premium options
- Useful for multi-screen window management
- Labeled keys beat mouse buttons for repeatable workflows
- Cheap entry point for productivity gains

## My Notes

Relevant for optimizing daily workflow (user's priority). A cheap macro pad could speed up repeated actions in network admin and development work.
