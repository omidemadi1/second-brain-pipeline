## Summary

Firecrawl PDF Inspector is the fastest PDF processing engine at 0.002 seconds per page. A fully local parser (not OCR) written in Rust. Supports Farsi text. Competes with Docling, Marker, and MinerU but focuses on parsing rather than OCR. Online demo available. 13k stars. Good for extracting structured text from PDFs quickly and locally.

## Key Takeaways

- 0.002 seconds per page — extremely fast
- Fully local, no OCR dependency
- Rust-based, 13k stars
- Farsi language support
- Smart routing: detects scanned vs text-based PDFs
- Competes with Docling/Marker/MinerU
