# Freedium — Paywall Bypass for Major News Sites

## Summary

Freedium is a web-based tool that lets you read paywalled articles from major publications for free — including Medium, NYT, WaPo, Bloomberg, Reuters, Economist, and Financial Times. Simply paste any paywalled article URL and Freedium fetches an open, ad-free version. Over 919K articles have been unlocked by the community. It maintains a trending feed and RSS, so you can follow popular reads. Useful for researchers, students, and anyone who needs quick access to paywalled journalism without subscriptions.

## Key Takeaways

- Paste any paywalled URL to get a free, ad-free version
- Supports Medium, NYT, WaPo, Bloomberg, Reuters, Economist, Financial Times
- Community-driven: 919K+ articles already unlocked
- Has trending section and RSS feed for following popular articles
- Simple interface — no installation or registration required
- Mirror at freedium-mirror.cfd for access reliability
