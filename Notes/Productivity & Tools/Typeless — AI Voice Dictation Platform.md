# Typeless — AI Voice Dictation Platform

## Summary
Typeless (typeless.com) is an AI voice dictation tool described as "the smartest AI voice dictation app ever built." The original tweet (641 likes) praises its ability to convert Persian speech to text and then intelligently structure the output — organizing content into paragraphs, bullet points, and even formatting automatically. Unlike basic STT that just transcribes, Typeless uses AI to *polish* the raw speech into well-formatted messages, emails, and documents that read as if carefully typed. A free alternative with similar features would be the biggest value for Iranian users; the author asked for recommendations on free equivalents.

## Key Takeaways
- AI-enhanced dictation: not just transcription, but intelligent structuring of text.
- Speech → paragraphs, bullet points, organized output (not raw transcript).
- Supports Persian (Farsi) speech input.
- Commercial product — the tweet asked for free alternatives.
- Target use case: speaking at your microphone, getting polished written output in seconds.

## My Notes
Competitor to Handy (offline). The key difference: Typeless does *post-processing* of transcript to polish writing. Handy is pure offline STT. For the VPS, Groq Whisper API remains the best option for transcription. Typeless is a commercial web service — less privacy-focused. Worth noting the "free alternatives" angle: this is a gap the market has.
