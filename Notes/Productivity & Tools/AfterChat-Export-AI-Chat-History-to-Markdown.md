## Summary

AfterChat is an open-source tool (11 stars, JavaScript) that exports chat history from major LLM platforms — ChatGPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, and others — to Markdown files. It preserves reasoning traces and citations, and supports bulk export of multiple conversations. Solves the fragmentation problem where AI knowledge gets scattered across platforms. Output is clean Markdown suitable for Obsidian, Notion, or any knowledge base.

## Key Takeaways

- **Multi-platform export** — ChatGPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, Claude, others
- **Preserves reasoning & citations** — Not just messages, but chain-of-thought
- **Markdown output** — Native Obsidian/Notion/knowledge-base compatible
- **Bulk export** — Multiple conversations at once
- **Data portability** — Breaks vendor lock-in for AI conversations
- **Open-source** — JavaScript, extensible
- **11 stars** — Early project, active development
