## Summary

**Google Search Console** now supports adding **Instagram, TikTok, X (Twitter), and YouTube** accounts directly within the platform. This is a significant update that brings social media presence into Google's official webmaster/search analytics tool. Previously, Search Console was limited to websites and web properties — this expansion acknowledges the growing importance of social media content in search visibility and digital presence management.

This means creators and businesses can now manage their social account connections alongside their website properties in one centralized dashboard, potentially enabling better cross-platform search insights and content optimization.

## Key Takeaways

- **What**: Google Search Console now accepts social media accounts
- **Platforms supported**: Instagram, TikTok, X (Twitter), YouTube
- **Significance**: Bridges the gap between web properties and social media in Google's tools
- **URL**: search.google.com/search-console/welcome
- **Implication**: Google increasingly treats social profiles as first-class web properties
