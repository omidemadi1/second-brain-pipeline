# PolyLayout — AI 3D Building Reconstruction from Images

## Summary
PolyLayout is a new AI system that reconstructs the 3D layout of an entire building from images alone — not one room at a time, but connected indoor spaces in a single pass. Most existing systems map individual rooms separately and stitch the results, which breaks at doorways. PolyLayout reconstructs multiple connected rooms at once so the whole floor plan stays coherent. It combines neural networks with real geometric reasoning, remains accurate across different scenes and camera setups, and represents a meaningful step toward machines that truly understand spatial environments and their position within them.

## Key Takeaways
- Reconstructs full 3D floor plans from images — not room-by-room stitching.
- Single-pass reconstruction of multiple connected rooms (handles doorways/transitions).
- Combines neural networks + explicit geometric reasoning for accuracy.
- Robust across different scenes and camera setups.
- Key innovation: coherent whole-building layout vs. fragmented room-by-room approaches.
- Significant for applications: robotics, AR/VR, architectural surveys, disaster response.
