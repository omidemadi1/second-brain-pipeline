## Summary

Trae is ByteDance's AI-powered IDE built on VS Code, offering a visual development environment similar to Cursor. It features a "Builder Mode" for guided development and a "SOLO Mode" for autonomous coding. The platform supports multiple AI models including Claude and GPT, and offers a free or low-cost tier, making it a potential Cursor competitor for developers seeking AI-assisted coding.

## Key Takeaways

- **Cursor competitor**: Trae positions itself as a direct alternative to Cursor with similar AI-powered IDE features, backed by ByteDance's resources
- **Multiple AI models**: Supports both Claude and GPT models, giving developers flexibility in choosing their AI backend
- **Two modes**: Builder Mode (guided, collaborative coding) and SOLO Mode (autonomous code generation) — the dual-mode approach caters to different workflow preferences
- **Free tier**: Unlike Cursor which requires a subscription, Trae offers a free or cheap tier, potentially making AI-powered coding more accessible
