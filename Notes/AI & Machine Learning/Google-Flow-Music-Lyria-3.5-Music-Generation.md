## Summary

**What:** Google's Flow Music platform powered by Lyria 3.5, a state-of-the-art music generation model that creates full songs with lyrics, instrumentation, and vocal performance from a single prompt.

**Key features:**
- Analyzes images and generates complete songs with rich musicality and dynamic vocals
- AI-powered Covers feature to restyle existing tracks
- Lip-sync music video generation using Google's Veo model
- Vibe-code audio plugins, music games, and custom DAWs directly in the browser
- Built-in stem splitting, audio effects, and remixing tools

**Use case:** Musicians, content creators, and producers who want to rapidly prototype songs, generate background music, or experiment with AI-assisted composition. Free to start with daily credits.

> Best for: anyone exploring AI music creation without needing traditional DAW skills.

## Key Takeaways

- Lyria 3.5 represents Google DeepMind's most capable music model with significantly improved vocal quality and musicality
- Flow Music is a complete creative platform — not just generation, but editing, video creation, and sharing
- The "Covers" feature allows restyling any track in a different genre or mood
- Veo integration enables automatic music video creation from generated songs
- Available as a web app with a dedicated iOS app
