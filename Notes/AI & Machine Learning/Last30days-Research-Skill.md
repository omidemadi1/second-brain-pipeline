## Summary

**What:** Last30days is an AI agent skill that researches any topic across Reddit, X/Twitter, YouTube, Hacker News, Polymarket, and the web, then synthesizes a grounded summary.

**Key features:**
- Multi-platform research: Reddit, X, YouTube, HN, Polymarket, web
- Recency filtering — focuses on last 30 days of content
- Synthesizes findings into structured summaries
- Works as a skill for Claude Code, OpenClaw, and similar agents
- 55k+ stars — one of the most popular agent skills
- Python-based, easy to customize

**Use case:** Anyone who needs current, multi-source research on any topic without manually checking multiple platforms.

> This is "deep research" as a reusable agent skill — check multiple platforms, synthesize, deliver.

## Key Takeaways

- Multi-platform approach provides comprehensive coverage
- Recency filtering ensures findings are current and relevant
- The skill format makes it composable with other agent workflows
- Polymarket integration adds prediction market insights
- 55k stars indicate strong reliability and community trust
- Can be customized for specific research domains
