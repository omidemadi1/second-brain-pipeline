## Summary

**What:** Grok Build Mode is a new feature by SpaceXAI for Grok 4.5 that lets you build working apps directly in chat — describe what you want, it builds, previews, and iterates live.

**Key features:**
- Describe what you want → Grok builds a working version live in chat
- Supports websites, dashboards, games, 3D worlds, calculators
- No IDE, terminal, or laptop needed — works on web, iOS, and Android
- Publish via grok.me or custom domain
- Available in Early Beta for SuperGrok Heavy subscribers
- Iterative refinement through conversation

**Use case:** Anyone who wants to quickly prototype and publish apps without traditional development tools or skills.

> This is the "describe it and it exists" paradigm for app creation.

## Key Takeaways

- Build Mode removes all tooling barriers — just describe and iterate
- Works across web and mobile (iOS/Android) from day one
- Publishing to grok.me or custom domains enables instant sharing
- The iterative conversation model allows rapid refinement
- Limited to SuperGrok Heavy subscribers in Early Beta
- Grok 4.5's improved code generation powers the builds

## My Notes

- This competes with v0, Bolt, and similar "prompt-to-app" tools
- The cross-platform support (web + mobile) is impressive
- Worth testing when broader access is available
- The publishing feature is particularly valuable for sharing prototypes
- Could be useful for rapid prototyping before committing to full development
