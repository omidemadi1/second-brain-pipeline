## Summary

**What:** Wan2GP is a free, open-source AI video studio that runs on as little as 6GB VRAM, supporting multiple video generation models including Wan 2.2, LTX-2, Hunyuan Video, and Flux.

**Key features:**
- Runs on 6GB VRAM — works on RTX 10 series and laptops
- Built-in models: Wan 2.2, LTX-2, Hunyuan Video, Flux, Qwen Image
- Text-to-video and image-to-video capabilities
- LoRA support for fine-tuned models
- Mask editor and prompt enhancer
- Browser-based UI with queue system
- No upload, no watermark, completely local

**Use case:** Creators and developers who want to generate AI videos locally without expensive hardware or cloud services.

> The 6GB VRAM requirement makes this accessible to a much wider audience than most video AI tools.

## Key Takeaways

- 6GB VRAM threshold includes older GPUs and laptops
- Multiple model support means flexibility in video style and quality
- LoRA support enables custom fine-tuned video generation
- Browser UI makes it accessible without terminal knowledge
- Queue system allows batch processing
- Completely local — no data leaves your machine
