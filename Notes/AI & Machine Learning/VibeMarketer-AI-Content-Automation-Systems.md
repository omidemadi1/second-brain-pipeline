## Summary

J.B. (@VibeMarketer_) shares a comprehensive article on building autonomous AI content systems that replace traditional $50k+ production teams. The piece details how high-performing AI teams ($1M+/mo) don't just prompt better — they build **systems for what happens after the prompt**. The framework uses Hermes kanban (multi-agent feature from Nous Research) to monitor 50+ X accounts, newsletters, subreddits, and YouTube channels nightly for emerging trends, recurring themes, and relevant news. The system employs a three-agent content team: one on search intent and competitive gaps, one drafting based on findings, and one running a recursive quality loop on every piece before it ships. Key insight: treat AI content like software — version control, code review, and CI/CD pipelines for content.

## Key Takeaways

- **Post-prompt systems beat better prompts** — The teams making $1M+/mo with AI video have a system for what happens after generation, not just better prompting skills
- **Multi-agent content pipeline** — Search agent → Draft agent → Quality agent (recursive loop) mirrors software dev: research → write → review → merge
- **Hermes kanban for trend monitoring** — Nightly scans across 50+ sources (X, newsletters, Reddit, YouTube) feed the ideation engine automatically
- **Recursive quality loops** — Every piece goes through multiple quality passes before publishing; "you don't write one draft and publish it"
- **Company Brain concept** — Shared prompts, decisions, corrections, and workflows become the default for every person AND AI agent on the team
- **n8n + ChatGPT + Bolt stack** — Workflows turned into real apps you can use, test, or sell; replaces hours of strategy, copywriting, and trendspotting
- **Content as code** — Version control, review cycles, and automated gates applied to content production
