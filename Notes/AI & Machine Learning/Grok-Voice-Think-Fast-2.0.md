## Summary

**What:** Grok Voice Think Fast 2.0 is xAI's next-generation speech-to-speech voice model with improved intelligence, transcription accuracy, and conversational capabilities.

**Key features:**
- 22% smoother conversation flow
- 44% faster first voice response time
- 40% better speech-to-text accuracy
- 60% lower inference token cost
- 1.5–2× transcription WER improvement on 24-language evaluation
- Pricing: $0.08 per audio minute (60% more than v1.0)

**Use case:** Developers building voice-enabled AI applications, conversational assistants, or real-time voice interactions who need fast, accurate, and cost-effective speech processing.

> The 60% inference cost reduction makes this viable for production voice applications at scale.

## Key Takeaways

- Significant improvement in both quality and efficiency over v1.0
- The 60% lower inference cost offsets the 60% price increase, resulting in net savings
- 24-language evaluation shows strong multilingual capabilities
- Snappier tool timing makes it suitable for voice-controlled agent workflows
- Available via API with `grok-voice-latest` endpoint

## My Notes

- Competitive with OpenAI's voice models and Google's speech APIs
- The cost structure makes it attractive for high-volume voice applications
- Worth testing for voice integration in Hermes or similar agents
- The transcription improvements are particularly notable
