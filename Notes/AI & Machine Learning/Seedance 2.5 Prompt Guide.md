## Summary

Seedance 2.5 Prompt Guide for AI video generation best practices. Treat prompts like a film script: define roles for reference images, split scenes into time segments, specify static elements, separate audio/dialogue/music. The structured approach — breaking video generation into temporal segments with explicit audio tracks — produces better results than monolithic prompts. Applies to Seedance 2.5 and other video generation models.

## Key Takeaways

- Treat video prompts like film scripts
- Define roles for reference images
- Split scenes into time segments
- Specify static vs dynamic elements separately
- Separate audio, dialogue, and music tracks
- Applicable beyond Seedance to other video models

## My Notes

Useful technique for any AI video generation work. The film script approach could be adapted to other video models like FLUX 3 or Sora.
