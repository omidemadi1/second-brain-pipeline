## Summary

**What:** OKF v0.2 (Open Knowledge Format) is Google's metadata format for AI-readable content, adding five trust signals: Provenance, Trust, Freshness, Lifecycle, and Attestation.

**Key features:**
- Five key metadata fields: Provenance, Trust, Freshness, Lifecycle, Attestation
- Answers critical questions about content from frontmatter alone
- Vendor-neutral, open specification
- Designed for enterprise AI agent workflows
- Plain Markdown format for easy adoption
- Potential SEO ranking signal (hypothesis)

**Use case:** Content creators, SEO professionals, and enterprises who want their content to be trustworthy and discoverable by AI agents.

> This could become the standard for content verification in the AI era.

## Key Takeaways

- Five trust signals address the core problem: "should I trust this content?"
- The format is deliberately minimal and opinionated-free
- Enterprise focus: solves the "agents consuming agents' output" trust problem
- Potential SEO implications make this worth monitoring for web publishers
- Plain Markdown format lowers adoption barriers
- Complements traditional structured data (schema.org) for AI consumption
