## Summary

AutoSocial is a free, open-source dashboard that automates the entire video content publishing workflow on your own computer. It replaces paid social media management tools ($30-200/month) with a fully local solution. Key features include auto-publishing to TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube; multi-account/multi-brand management; smart scheduling with cron; per-account/platform queues; video download via yt-dlp; video processing with FFmpeg (logo overlay, variant creation); and session persistence for login reuse. Everything runs locally, keeping your data and credentials private.

## Key Takeaways

- Replaces $30-200/month social media management tools with a free local solution
- Supports TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube with multi-account management
- Uses yt-dlp for video download and FFmpeg for processing/customization
- Session storage means you only log in once per account
- Full local execution = complete privacy and control over credentials
- Ideal for content creators, indie hackers, and AI product builders
