## Summary
Thread listing 10 open-source GitHub repos that can be monetized: Cal.com (open-source Calendly, fork and white-label for $200/mo), Plausible Analytics (privacy-first GA, self-host and resell at $50/mo/client), Ghost (open-source Substack alternative), and others. Shows how open-source can be bootstrapped into profitable businesses through hosting, white-labeling, and reselling.

## Key Takeaways
- Open-source software can be monetized by offering managed hosting, white-labeling, and reselling — you don't need to build from scratch
- Cal.com is an open-source alternative to Calendly that can be forked and white-labeled for ~$200/mo recurring revenue
- Plausible Analytics is a privacy-first alternative to Google Analytics — self-host and resell to clients at ~$50/mo each
- Ghost is an open-source Substack alternative that can be used to build paid newsletter businesses
- The key business model is taking care of hosting, support, and infrastructure for non-technical customers who want the software without the DevOps
