## Summary

Google's official SEO documentation hub covering how Google crawls, indexes, and ranks websites. The SEO Starter Guide provides best practices for making content search-friendly. Key sections include: getting your website on Google (URL structure, duplicate content, canonical URLs), improving how your site appears (controlling crawling/indexing, structured data for rich results, JavaScript SEO), and monitoring performance with Search Console. The documentation also covers Search Console setup, traffic debugging, and training resources. Essential reading for anyone managing online content visibility.

## Key Takeaways

- SEO is the process of increasing website visibility on search engines to attract relevant traffic
- The SEO Starter Guide covers best practices for crawling, indexing, and understanding content
- Search Console is the primary tool for monitoring site performance on Google Search
- Key topics: URL structure, duplicate content handling, canonical URLs, structured data
- JavaScript SEO is a dedicated section — important for modern web apps
- Structured data enables rich results (enhanced search listings)
- The guide is meant for anyone who owns, manages, monetizes, or promotes online content
- Google provides training video series and case studies alongside documentation
