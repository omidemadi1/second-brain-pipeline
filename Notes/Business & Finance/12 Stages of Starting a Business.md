## Summary

Infographic covering 12 stages of starting a business (مراحل راهاندازی یک کسبوکار) — from idea selection to launch. The stages: (1) Idea selection, (2) Market research, (3) Business model, (4) Brand naming, (5) Business plan, (6) Legal registration, (7) Licenses, (8) Admin setup, (9) Finances, (10) Team building, (11) Sales preparation, (12) Launch. A comprehensive checklist for launching any business.

## Key Takeaways

- 12-step framework from idea to launch
- Covers legal, financial, operational, and marketing aspects
- Market research comes before business model
- Brand naming is step 4, not step 1
- Legal registration and licenses are separate steps
- Team building comes before sales preparation

## My Notes

Good reference for structuring the wardrobe app launch. Useful as a checklist to ensure no critical steps are missed. The ordering is instructive — many entrepreneurs jump to team building before having a validated business model.
