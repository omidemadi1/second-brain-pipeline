## Summary

Ethan Cajigas (@aureliuscajigas), a 20-year-old indie app builder, shares his journey of scaling two apps to $240,000/year in revenue through paid advertising. After initially relying on organic content and "building in public" which yielded $0 MRR, he discovered that paid ads were the key growth lever. His first app hit $20,000/month after implementing paid ads. The article details the tactical shift from hope-based organic marketing to systematic paid acquisition, covering ad creative testing, targeting, and unit economics that made the campaigns profitable.

## Key Takeaways

- **Organic ≠ Growth**: Building in public and posting content alone won't drive revenue; paid acquisition is often necessary for scalable growth
- **Paid Ads as a Science**: Treat ad spend as an investment portfolio — test creatives, audiences, and hooks systematically
- **Unit Economics First**: Know your LTV and CAC before scaling spend; profitable unit economics enable aggressive reinvestment
- **Creative is King**: The ad creative (hook, visual, copy) matters more than targeting; test dozens of variations
- **Speed of Iteration**: Fast feedback loops from paid ads accelerate product-market fit discovery vs. slow organic growth
- **Age is Irrelevant**: At 20, Ethan proves execution beats credentials; the market only cares about results

## My Notes

This resonates with the AI wardrobe styling app project — we have $0 marketing budget but the lesson is clear: organic-only is a trap. Need to allocate even a small test budget ($100-500) to validate paid channels early. The "build boring apps that solve annoying problems" philosophy aligns with our approach.
