## Summary

Karoon is an Iranian crypto research community co-founded by Sabir. Their three goals: (1) Find projects worth your time, (2) Recommend tokens with real utility (not scams), (3) Upgrade the community's collective financial literacy. Covers crypto, gold, commodities, forex, and Iranian/US stock markets. Telegram group available. Active since 2017, focusing on the last 4 years.

## Key Takeaways

- Iranian crypto research community since 2017
- Focus on legitimate projects, not scams
- Covers multiple asset classes: crypto, gold, forex, stocks
- Goal: upgrade community financial literacy
- Telegram group: t.me/Karoongruop
