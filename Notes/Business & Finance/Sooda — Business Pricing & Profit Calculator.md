# Sooda — Business Pricing & Profit Calculator

## Summary
Sooda (سودا) is a free open-source tool for calculating profit, selling price and discounts for businesses. It was shared by Persian GitHub as a practical utility that helps business owners do accurate pricing in seconds, avoiding the common mistake of mispricing that eats into profit margins. The repo (github.com/Mahdi-mortazavi/sooda) is a lightweight web app / PWA — enter purchase price + desired profit percentage and it computes the numbers, with one-tap native install on Android/desktop and home-screen shortcuts on iPhone. The post had strong engagement: 153 likes, 10 retweets.

## Key Takeaways
- Mispricing silently destroys a large share of business profit — accurate, fast pricing matters.
- Sooda is a free calculator for: profit, selling price, and discounts.
- Works as a PWA with one-tap install on Android/desktop and an illustrated add-to-home-screen guide on iPhone.
- Input model is simple: purchase price + desired profit % → suggested selling price.
- Persian-language project, relevant to Iranian small businesses and e-commerce sellers.
- Repo: https://github.com/Mahdi-mortazavi/sooda
