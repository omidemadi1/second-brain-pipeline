## Summary

Mau Baron (@maubaron) is the solo developer behind Prayer Lock, a Christian focus app ranked #7 in the Reference category on app stores. He bootstrapped the app from zero to $50K-$100K/month revenue and is scaling toward $1M/month. The tweet gained 791 likes and 58 retweets, reflecting strong interest in his bootstrapping journey. His LinkedIn post reveals he disappeared for a year to build the app, and it now makes $20K+/month. The app offers in-app purchases and has a 4.7-star rating on Google Play.

## Key Takeaways

- **Solo founder, bootstrapped**: No VC funding — built and scaled independently.
- **$50K-$100K/month revenue**: From a niche Christian app in the Reference category.
- **Scaling to $1M/month**: Ambitious growth target from a solo operation.
- **4.7-star rating**: High user satisfaction indicates strong product-market fit.
- **Niche dominance**: #7 in Reference category — proves niche apps can generate serious revenue.
- **Zero-budget growth model**: Worth studying for the wardrobe app's $0 marketing budget constraint.
- **791 likes on tweet**: Strong social proof and community interest in bootstrapping stories.
