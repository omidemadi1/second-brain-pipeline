## Summary

A comprehensive comparison table of 10 financing methods, originally in Farsi, from invexholding.co. Each method is evaluated by suitable use case, main advantage, and key weakness.

**Methods compared:**
1. **Personal Capital (سرمایه شخصی)** — Best for early-stage, full control, limited by personal wealth
2. **Bank Loan (وام بانکی)** — Best for established businesses, low cost, hard to qualify
3. **Government Grants (کمک‌های دولتی)** — Best for specific sectors, non-dilutive, competitive
4. **Venture Capital (سرمایه‌گذاری خطرپذیر)** — Best for high-growth startups, large amounts, dilutive
5. **Capital Increase (افزایش سرمایه)** — Best for listed companies, existing shareholders, dilutive
6. **Murabaha Bonds (اوراق مرابحه)** — Best for asset-backed financing, Sharia-compliant, complex
7. **Leasing Bonds (اوراق اجاره)** — Best for equipment/asset acquisition, tax benefits, asset-dependent
8. **Fund (صندوق)** — Best for diversified investment, professional management, fees
9. **Crowdfunding (تامین مالی جمعی)** — Best for early products, community building, limited scale
10. **IPO** — Best for mature companies, maximum capital, regulatory burden

## Key Takeaways

- No single financing method is universally best — the right choice depends on company stage, sector, and goals
- Early-stage companies should consider personal capital, crowdfunding, or grants
- Growth-stage companies should look at VC or bank loans
- Mature companies can access capital markets via bonds or IPOs
- Each method has trade-offs between cost, control, dilution, and accessibility
