Sooda is a free offline-first PWA pricing calculator for businesses. Calculates profit, selling price, discounts, and profit margins. Simple UI, bilingual (English/Farsi), zero server dependencies. 14 stars. Good for quick pricing calculations for small businesses and freelancers.
