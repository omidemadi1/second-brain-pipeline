## Summary

A 6-hour design challenge: Claude Code (equipped with the "Impeccable" design skill by ex-Googler Paul + Magnific MCP for asset generation) vs. a human designer with 20 years of experience. Both receive the same brief — design a website for "Steel & Stone," a one-person knife sharpening service in Asheville, NC. The founder sharpens kitchen knives on traditional whetstones; voice is quiet, precise, craft-obsessed, not salesy. Target audience: home cooks and small restaurants. Assets: two mediocre photos (workshop + founder).

The video documents the entire process. Claude reads the brief strategically (identifies it as a persuasion problem, not a portfolio piece), generates 7 visual directions, and builds a complete site with animations, parallax, scroll-driven knife reveal, and custom interactions. The human designer works in parallel, also using Magnific for visuals. Final comparison reveals both produce exceptional work — Claude's site is technically impressive with sophisticated interactions (sliding knife animation, parallax reveals, hover states), while the human's site shows more refined craft details (custom diagonal line on "5" icon matching animation language). The human designer concludes: "If you told me a few years ago I could create this in 6 hours including visuals and video, I'd be blown away."

## Key Takeaways

- **Strategic Thinking > Pixel Pushing**: Both AI and pro started with positioning — "persuasion problem, not portfolio piece" — avoiding the "Asheville Craftsman" cliché (cream bg, serif, terracotta)
- **Impeccable Skill = Anti-Slop Guardrails**: The skill provides 50k+ GitHub stars of design language constraints — no AI-default fonts, spacing, colors; forces intentional choices
- **7 Directions > 1 Direction**: Exploring 7 distinct visual strategies (wetstone green, molten steel, minimalist, etc.) before committing beats single-path iteration
- **Motion as Brand Language**: The sliding knife animation becomes the through-line — hover states, scroll reveals, button interactions all speak the same kinetic vocabulary
- **Magnific MCP = Asset Superpower**: Both designers used Magnific for custom visuals; AI-generated photography/illustration removes the "bad asset" bottleneck
- **Human Craft Still Wins on Micro-Details**: The diagonal line on the "5" icon matching the knife's animation angle — that intentionality is currently human territory
- **6 Hours for Production-Ready**: Both delivered launch-quality sites with CMS-ready components, animations, responsive behavior — the new baseline for design velocity

## My Notes

6. The "book of sharpening" section → our "wardrobe guide" or "styling principles" educational content
