## Summary

**Magnific Auto Layers** is an AI-powered tool that automatically separates any image into its constituent layers — text, subjects, and background — enabling individual editing without rebuilding the entire design. Drop any image and get clean separation of elements as editable layers.

Key capabilities:
- **Layer decomposition**: Splits images into text, subject, and background layers
- **Individual editing**: Move, delete, or modify each layer independently
- **Available in**: Magnific Designer, Spaces, and via Magnific MCP
- **Use case**: Fix typos in designs, swap backgrounds, reposition subjects without starting from scratch

Part of the broader Magnific AI suite which includes upscaling, resizing, and other AI-powered image tools. The tool was announced June 2026 and is available in the Magnific web app.
