## Summary

TypeUI Design Extractor is a Chrome extension with 30k installs and 5 stars. It converts any website's design into SKILL/DESIGN.md prompts that AI tools can use to replicate the design. Works with Claude, Cursor, OpenCode, Windsurf, and more. Browse to any site, extract its design system, and feed it to your coding agent.

## Key Takeaways

- Chrome extension, 30k installs, 5 stars
- Extracts any website's design into SKILL/DESIGN.md prompts
- Compatible with Claude, Cursor, OpenCode, Windsurf
- Turns visual designs into replicable AI instructions
- Quick way to capture design inspiration
