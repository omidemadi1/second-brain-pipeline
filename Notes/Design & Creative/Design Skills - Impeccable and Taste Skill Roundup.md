## Summary

A curated pairing of two design guide skills: Impeccable (impeccable.style) and Taste Skill (tasteskill.dev). Both are guide files that teach AI to produce better, less cliché UI designs. Impeccable offers 1 skill with 23 commands and curated anti-patterns, tuned on frontier models. Taste Skill v2 reads the brief, infers design direction, and ships non-templated interfaces. Together they represent the two leading anti-slop design frameworks for AI coding tools.

## Key Takeaways

- Impeccable: 23 commands, anti-patterns, tuned on frontier models
- Taste Skill: brief inference, strict pre-flight checks, 13 sub-skills
- Both work with Cursor, Claude Code, Codex, Windsurf
- Complementary approaches to AI design quality
- Both free and open-source

## My Notes

Should evaluate both side-by-side for the wardrobe app UI work. They emphasize different aspects — Impeccable focuses on design language and commands, Taste Skill on brief inference and visual style families.
