# Google AI Tools for Ads & Analytics (2026)

## Summary
Google is rolling out new AI and agentic capabilities for Google Ads and Google Analytics, designed to automate repetitive marketing tasks and free marketers to focus on strategy. The new features help marketers automate campaign planning, execution, and analysis workflows. AI plays a more prominent role in ad campaigns and data analysis, enabling smarter decision-making. The shift positions AI as a core part of the entire marketing pipeline — from planning through execution to analysis — rather than just a surface-level feature add-on.

## Key Takeaways
- New AI tools in Google Ads + Google Analytics focus on automation of repetitive tasks.
- "Agentic" capabilities: AI agents that can act autonomously on marketing workflows.
- Goal: streamline the full pipeline — planning → execution → analysis.
- AI is becoming central to ad campaign optimization and data-driven decisions.
- Marketers can redirect time saved on busywork toward higher-level strategy.
