## Summary

Jurre (@jurree), building a collection app empire (ex-Amazon), achieved ~32,000 new organic users for his iOS app Gotcha (an animal identifier) in just one week with $0 ad spend. He shared a detailed breakdown of the strategy that drove this growth.

**Gotcha** — AI-powered animal identifier app on iOS (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gotcha-animal-identifier/id6778118327)

## Key Takeaways

- 32k new app users in 1 week, entirely organic — no paid advertising
- The strategy appears to be documented in an attached article/thread
- Demonstrates that viral organic growth is achievable for niche utility apps
- Animal identification is a specific, shareable use case that drives word-of-mouth

## My Notes

This is a strong case study in organic user acquisition for mobile apps.
