## Summary

Brand Touchpoints infographic (نقاط تماس برند) covering 9 touchpoint categories around a brand: (1) Identity — logo, colors, messaging, (2) Digital Presence — website, social media, email, (3) Marketing — advertising, content, PR, (4) Customer Experience — support, onboarding, feedback, (5) Purchase — checkout, pricing, payment, (6) Products & Services — quality, features, delivery, (7) Human Management — team culture, training, values, (8) Community & Trust — reviews, testimonials, partnerships, (9) Physical Environment — office, packaging, retail. Each with detailed sub-categories.

## Key Takeaways

- 9 comprehensive brand touchpoint categories
- Identity → Digital → Marketing → Experience → Purchase → Product → People → Community → Physical
- Every interaction point matters — not just marketing
- Physical environment is as important as digital
- Community and trust are distinct from marketing
- Useful audit framework for brand consistency

## My Notes

Good framework for auditing the wardrobe app's brand presence across all touchpoints. Useful for planning a comprehensive launch strategy that covers all 9 categories.
