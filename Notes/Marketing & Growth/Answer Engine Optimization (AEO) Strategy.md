## Summary

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) is how brands get recommended by AI assistants. Key concepts: AI assistants use "query fan" — a single prompt gets broken into dozens of sub-searches behind the scenes (ChatGPT deep research ran 420 searches for one prompt). Three factors drive AI citations: (1) Consensus — brand appears in many places saying the same thing, (2) Freshness — AI-cited content is 25.7% fresher than Google results, (3) Authority — 76% of AI overview citations come from Google top-10 pages. Strategy: brand gap analysis (missed citations, competitor gaps, topic gaps), 30-second technical check, measurement from day one, refresh outdated content, get listed in listicles/reviews/Reddit. AI traffic grew 9.7x in the past year.

## Key Takeaways

- Query fan: AI breaks one prompt into dozens of sub-searches
- Three factors: consensus, freshness, authority
- 76% of AI citations come from Google top-10 pages
- AI-cited content is 25.7% fresher than traditional results
- Brand gap analysis reveals missed citation opportunities
- AI traffic grew 9.7x in the past year
- Strategy: refresh outdated content, get in listicles, create YouTube content
