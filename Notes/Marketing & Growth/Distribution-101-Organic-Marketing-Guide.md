## Summary

EP (@eptwts) published a comprehensive guide titled "Distribution 101: How to Sell Your Products" — an organic marketing framework covering the three core distribution channels and the principles that make them work.

**The three channels:**

1. **X (Twitter)** — Build audience through niche engagement, valuable tweets, and trend leverage. Not self-promotion — value-first content that builds trust.
2. **YouTube** — Product education through video. SEO-optimized content that explains benefits and builds community around your niche.
3. **Short-form (TikTok + Reels)** — Reach younger/broader audiences with trending, quick, engaging content that drives traffic to your product.

**Core principles:**
- **1:1 Niche-Product Rule** — Target one specific niche where your product solves a clear problem. Avoid spray-and-pray.
- **VALUE Rule** — Content must provide real value (educational, entertaining, problem-solving). Value builds trust → trust leads to sales.
- **"Make every video a hidden VSL"** — Even educational content should subtly showcase product benefits.
- **Don't be a "greedy boy"** — Price fairly to build long-term trust.

## Key Takeaways

- Distribution matters as much as — or more than — product quality
- Three channels cover most audiences: X for niche, YouTube for education, short-form for reach
- The 1:1 niche-product rule prevents diluting your message across too many audiences
- Video is the most powerful distribution tool, especially short-form
- Community building and trust are the foundation of organic sales
- Multi-channel approach recommended — don't rely on a single platform

## My Notes

This is a solid organic marketing framework. The key insight is that distribution isn't about spamming — it's about providing value in the right places for the right audiences. The three-channel approach (X + YouTube + short-form) covers most digital audiences. The "hidden VSL" concept is clever — making every piece of content subtly work toward your product goal without being pushy.
