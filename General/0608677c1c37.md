**AutoSocial** — open-source dashboard for managing video content publishing on TikTok, Instagram, YouTube.

Features:
- Multi-account and multi-brand management
- Smart scheduling via Cron or specific time
- Separate queues per account and platform
- yt-dlp video download
- FFmpeg processing (adding logos, creating variant versions)
- Session reuse for login
- Runs fully local — complete data control and privacy

GitHub: https://github.com/Katzca/AutoSocial
