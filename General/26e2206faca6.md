## General

### Seedance 2.5 Prompt Guide

AI video generation best practices:

- **Treat prompts like a film script**
- Define roles for reference images
- Split scenes into time segments
- Specify static elements
- Separate audio/dialogue/music

Applies to Seedance 2.5 and other video models.
