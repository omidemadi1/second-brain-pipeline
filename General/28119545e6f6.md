## General

### Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) — 5 Key Pieces

**1. Understand AI search**
AI assistants use query fan-out (breaking one prompt into dozens of sub-queries). Content must be relevant across an entire topic, not just keywords.

**2. Three drivers of AI citation:**
- **Consensus:** brand mentioned in many places
- **Freshness:** AI cites content 25.7% fresher than Google top 10
- **Authority:** 76% of AI citations come from Google top 10 pages

**3. Brand gap analysis**
Measure where your brand appears vs where it should appear across Google, AI results, and web. Filter for missed citations, competitor gaps, topic gaps.

**4. Content strategy**
- Refresh outdated pages (fastest win)
- Get mentioned in listicles, reviews, Reddit
- Produce video content for YouTube

**5. Technical check**
- Ensure AI can crawl your site
- Set up measurements (AI traffic channel in GA4)
- Implement 'how did you hear about us' question

AI traffic grew 9.7x in past year. ChatGPT alone grown 85% since January. Most businesses haven't started AEO.
