# AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) - AI Search Strategy

## Summary
Comprehensive guide on optimizing for AI search engines (AEO). AI assistants don't work like Google - they use "query fan" to break prompts into dozens of smaller searches.

## Key Concepts

### Query Fan
- AI breaks one prompt into multiple specific searches
- Example: "Plan a 5-day trip to Japan" → searches for neighborhoods, weather, rail pass, etc.
- ChatGPT's deep research mode ran 420 searches for a single prompt

### Three Factors Driving AI Citations
1. **Consensus** - Brand mentions across multiple sources
2. **Freshness** - AI cites content 25.7% fresher than Google top 10
3. **Authority** - 76% of AI citations come from Google top 10 pages

### Brand Gap Analysis
- Measure where brand should show up vs. where it actually shows up
- Use tools like HRefs Brand Radar
- Identify: missed citation opportunities, competitor gaps, topic gaps

### Technical Setup
- 30-second technical check for AI crawling
- Implement measurement (GA4 AI traffic channel)
- "How did you hear about us" question
