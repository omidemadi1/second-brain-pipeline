# AutoSocial - Open Source Social Media Dashboard

## Summary
Free open-source dashboard for managing video content publishing across social media platforms. Replaces $30-200/month social media management tools.

## Features
- Auto-publish to TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube
- Multi-account and multi-brand management
- Smart scheduling with Cron or specific times
- Separate queues per account and platform
- Video download with yt-dlp
- Video processing and customization with FFmpeg (logos, versioning)
- Session storage and reuse
- Fully local execution for data control and privacy

## Who It's For
Content creators, Indie Hackers, AI product builders - saves hours of repetitive work.
