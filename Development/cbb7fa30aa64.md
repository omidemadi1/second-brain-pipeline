## Development

### Brand Touchpoints (نقاط تماس برند)

9 touchpoint categories around a brand:

1. **Identity** — Logo, name, visual language
2. **Digital Presence** — Website, social media, mobile apps
3. **Marketing** — Ads, content, campaigns
4. **Customer Experience** — Service, support, UX
5. **Purchase** — Buying process, checkout, payment
6. **Products & Services** — Core offerings, quality, packaging
7. **Human Management** — Team, hiring, culture
8. **Community & Trust** — Reviews, partnerships, CSR
9. **Physical Environment** — Stores, offices, events

Each with sub-categories.
